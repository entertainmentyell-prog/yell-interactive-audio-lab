// FIELD NOTES — THE FOUR FORMULAS

01 · NYQUISTfnyq = fs / 2

Tones above Nyquist don't disappear — they mirror back as aliases: fa = |fs·round(f/fs) − f|.

02 · BIT DEPTHSNR ≈ 6.02·b + 1.76 dB

Every extra bit buys ~6 dB of headroom and halves the step size Δ = 2/2b.

03 · DITHERnoise ↑ · distortion ↓

~1–2 LSB of random noise turns correlated, harmonic quantization error into a benign, steady floor.

04 · LATENCYt ≈ 2N / fs + driver

Round-trip = in-buffer + out-buffer. Halve N for feel, double it for CPU safety — never both.
