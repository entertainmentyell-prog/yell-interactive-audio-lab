Phase Wheel

5 interactive sims · rotation, offset, time delay, XY phase & comb filtering

Every sine wave begins as a rotation. A point spinning on the phase wheel casts a vertical shadow that traces a sine — the angle of the point at any instant is the phase. The scrub slider sets the phase (even mid-play, the wheel resumes from where you place it); the display card shows the live angle.

A signal added to a delayed copy of itself. Where the delay equals a half-period (180° on the wheel) the two cancel; where it equals a whole period they double. Switch to pink noise to hear the comb as a hollow tonal coloration; sweep the delay by hand and you've built a flanger.
